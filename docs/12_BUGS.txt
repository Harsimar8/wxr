------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUG 1  F-FACTOR USES GROUND SPEED FOR BOTH PARTS                (medium)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE:  radar.py, _f_factor_hazard()
            v = max(speed_kt, 60.0) * 0.5144
            f = gradient(tail) * v / 9.81  -  up / v
        main.py, _update_pws() passes GROUND speed (gs_kt).

WHAT IS WRONG:
  The real formula has two parts:
     F = (tailwind change per second) / g   -   (down wind) / AIRSPEED
  - Part 1: how fast you fly INTO the wind change = GROUND speed.   OK
  - Part 2: how steep the down wind pushes you = AIRSPEED (TAS).    WRONG
  The app uses ground speed for both.

EXAMPLE:
  Approach at 140 kt airspeed with a 30 kt headwind -> ground speed 110.
  Part 2 becomes w / 110 instead of w / 140: about 27 % too strong.
  With a tailwind it is too weak. So the PWS alerts a bit early with a
  headwind, a bit late with a tailwind.

FIX:
  Pass both speeds: windshear(..., gs_kt=gs, tas_kt=tas).
      f = gradient(tail) * gs / g  -  up / tas
  FlightGear gives tas_kt already (the ND shows it).


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUG 2  FLIGHTGEAR SPEED 0 -> PWS USES THE MADE-UP 250 KT        (medium)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE:  main.py, _update_pws()
            speed = (self.fg.state.get("gs_kt") if fg_live else None)
                    or self.sim_gs

WHAT IS WRONG:
  "or" treats 0 as "no value". With FlightGear connected and the plane
  standing still (gs = 0), the PWS uses sim_gs instead, which is the
  Simulated-mode speed (250 kt at start). The F-factor part 1 grows with
  speed, so a storm ahead of a PARKED plane can give a windshear alert
  as if it were flying at 250 kt.

FIX:
  if fg_live: speed = fg.state.get("gs_kt", 0.0)   (0 stays 0)
  else:       speed = self.sim_gs
  (And bug 5: on the ground below take-off speed there should be no
  alert at all.)


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUG 3  LANDING OR TAKE-OFF IS GUESSED FROM VERTICAL SPEED ONLY    (small)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE:  main.py, _update_pws()
            elif vs_fps < -3.0:   -> "Go around, windshear ahead"
            else:                 -> "Windshear ahead, windshear ahead"

WHAT IS WRONG:
  Descending faster than 180 ft/min = landing, anything else = take-off.
  On an approach with a level part (e.g. level at 1,000 ft before
  the glide slope) the app says the TAKE-OFF message.
  A real system uses the flight phase (gear down, flaps, airspeed,
  radio altitude going down).

FIX:
  Read gear-down and flaps from FlightGear. Landing = gear down AND
  (descending OR flaps in landing position). Otherwise take-off.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUG 4  VOICE ALERT REPEATS WHEN THE ALERT BLINKS                  (small)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE:  main.py, _update_pws()
            if level not in ("warning", "caution"):
                self.pws_said = (None, 0.0)

WHAT IS WRONG:
  The PWS is checked 4 times a second. When the plane is at the edge
  of the alert area, the alert can go on - off - on. Every "off" clears
  what was said, so every "on" says it again: "Monitor radar display"
  many times in a few seconds.
  Real systems hold an alert for a few seconds once it is given.

FIX:
  Keep the alert for at least 3-5 seconds after it was last seen
  before clearing pws_said.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUG 5  NO "ALERTS OFF" ON THE RUNWAY                              (small)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE:  main.py, _update_pws()

WHAT IS WRONG:
  The real PWS switches alerts OFF:
    - on take-off from 100 kt until 50 ft (too late to stop, and the
      crew is busy),
    - on landing below 50 ft.
  The app alerts all the way to the ground and during the take-off run.
  The docs already describe these rules (03_windshear.txt), but the
  code does not do them.

FIX:
  agl < 50 ft  -> no new alert,
  on the ground and ground speed > 100 kt -> no new alert.

RISK 7  CLOUD SHAPES AND WEATHER STATIONS NEVER TESTED IN FLIGHTGEAR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not a bug found, but a place where bugs may hide. The guide itself says
(section 20): "Cloud shapes and stations: tested with made-up data, not
yet in real FlightGear." test_scenarios.py also could not be run here.
Things to watch when you test in FlightGear:
  - echoes at the right place on the clouds (not shifted),
  - hail icon and microburst on the storm's core,
  - freezing level per area changing smoothly between tiles,
  - nothing slow when many clouds are loaded.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHECKED AND NOT BUGS (so you don't look for them again)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Weak storms (FlightGear rain below about 0.5) show nothing in
    Simulated mode at high altitude: the beam goes over their tops.
    This is real (beam overshoot), not a bug. Tilt down to see them.
  - The windshear alert can take up to 0.25 s to appear after a
    microburst is added: it is checked 4 times a second, on purpose.
  - Wind arrow direction on the ND: correct (points where the wind
    blows to, turned to magnetic like the rest of the ND).
  - Volume shapes (circle, ellipse, box, rotation): same maths as
    FlightGear's own local_weather.nas.
  - Yellow PAC arc: on the right bearings, cleared when turned off.
  - Step 1 change (radar equation, beam smearing): echoes still at the
    right place (test_place_map ALL OK), no slowdown you can see.
